# NaijaMove

Fair local rides for Nigeria. Riders pay NaijaMove. Drivers are paid by the company. The fare cannot be bid down below the floor.

Hello is the inspected older car: air conditioner on, clean, and a responsible driver. Women and men both drive. Cars under 10 years take every class. Hello stops at 18 years.

Registration asks for a name, phone, NIN, and home address. A driver also submits those for a police check. The desk clears that submission before the car can go online. This preview stores the record on the phone. It does not call the police or a bank.
